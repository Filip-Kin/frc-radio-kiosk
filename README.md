# FRC radio kiosk

Bench box that powers and configures FRC radios: dual-mode PoE (802.3at via
LTC4279, passive 12 V via TPS26600), USB-C PD or DC in, 15.6 V out for the
Toughbook, 4x CR123A backup, STM32F072 firmware.

- `Radio_Kiosk_v2/` first full board, 100 x 130 mm
- `Radio_Kiosk_v2b/` hand-packed board, 84 x 133 mm, 2 oz copper (order that at JLC)
- `Radio_Kiosk_v2d/` 4-layer rework of v2c, 85 x 120 mm, In1 GND plane (see its NOTES.md)
- `Radio_Kiosk_v1/` the built board, 70 x 105 mm: USB-C PD 20 V in, XL4015 16 V laptop out, LM2596 12 V passive PoE. Board was drawn in EasyEDA Pro; `fab/` is its 2026-03-25 export (Gerbers, BOM, STEP, 3D shell). The KiCad schematic is an early draft, not the built circuit.
- each board dir: `board.kicad_sch`, `board.kicad_pcb`, `board.kicad_pro`, `board.loon.json`, `firmware/` (PlatformIO)

The schematic and board are generated by scripts in
[loon](https://github.com/Filip-Kin/loon): `scripts/build-radio-kiosk.ts`,
`build-radio-kiosk-pcb.ts` (v2), `build-radio-kiosk-pcb-v2b.ts`. Design notes
are on the internal wiki (`frc/radio-kiosk-pcb`).
